Why D61

At District 61, we are more than just a school district.

We are a community dedicated to fostering academic excellence, continual improvement, and a sense of belonging. As the largest school district between Salt Lake City, Utah, and Denver, Colorado, we support approximately 19,500 students and just shy of 3,500 staff. Committed to offering unparalleled opportunities for growth and learning, we believe in empowering our families with options in how and where they educate their children, and we want you to be involved in your child's journey here at D61. Together, we embrace diversity, innovation, and collaboration, and aim to engage, equip, and empower each and every student each and every day, ensuring they reach their full potential. 

Explore our district and discover how D61 can be the foundation for your child's educational journey.

Why Learn Here?

At District 61, we aim to engage, equip, and empower each and every student, each and every day. We are dedicated to delivering exceptional education through various pathways and opportunities for our students. We are committed to empowering students to explore their interests and excel academically, preparing them for college, career, and life.

Why Work Here?

Join District 61 for a fulfilling career where you can make a real difference in the lives of our students. With a focus on academic excellence, our collaborative environment offers professional growth and the chance to shape the future of education in Mesa County.

Why Live Here?

Discover the charm of Grand Junction on Colorado's Western Slope. As the largest city between Salt Lake City and Denver, Grand Junction combines natural beauty, culture, and community. From outdoor recreation, to a rich arts scene, and excellent shopping and dining, Grand Junction offers the best of Colorado living, where adventure awaits.
